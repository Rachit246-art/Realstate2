VÉLORA / ABOUT OUR HERITAGE

A Legacy Founded on Architectural Integrity

For over a quarter century, VÉLORA has redefined luxury residential development through purist design, sustainable environmental stewardship, and residences built to outlast eras.

FOUNDER'S PERSPECTIVE

“We Do Not Build Structures. We Create Legacies.”

When we established VÉLORA in 2001, the luxury landscape in India was dominated by ornate imitations. We took the opposite path: radical purity, honest natural materials, and living spaces that prioritize silence, light, and private well-being.

Each residence we sculpt represents thousands of hours of architectural refinement. We select micro-locations with the same scrutiny an art curator applies to rare masterpieces.

Kabir Vardhan
Founder & Chief Architectural Director

THE VÉLORA MANIFESTO

Four Pillars of Architectural Distinction

Every milestone residence in our portfolio adheres to our non-negotiable architectural doctrine.

01

Biophilic Resonance

Seamless integration with nature. Floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass, natural microclimates, and private suspended botanical sky terraces.

02

Harmonic Proportions

Golden-ratio spatial planning, minimum 12.5 ft ceiling volumes, and directional dual-aspect exposure providing natural light throughout the day.

03

Noble Materiality

Hand-selected Italian travertine, bespoke architectural bronze hardware, brushed aged brass, and FSC-certified mature teakwood.

04

Zero Net-Carbon

Geothermal cooling systems, rooftop solar integration, 100% rainwater harvesting, and IGBC Platinum sustainable compliance.

OUR MILESTONES

25 Years of Uncompromising Trust

From a boutique architectural studio in Delhi to India's foremost luxury property visionary.

2001

The Inception of VÉLORA

Founded with the conviction that Indian residential architecture deserved world-class purist modernism. Completed our first bespoke private villa in South Delhi.

2008

First Biophilic Masterplan Community

Pioneered NCR's first zero-emission residential sanctuary in Noida, weaving 70% open green reserves around custom architectural estates.

2016

Expansion to Bangalore & Mumbai

Inaugurated Lakeside Villas in Bangalore and The Aurum Penthouses in Worli, expanding our footprint to high-net-worth patrons nationwide.

2022
